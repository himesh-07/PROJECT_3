# Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Source:** Home Baker's Handbook, Chapter 4
**Yield:** ~24 cookies | **Prep:** 15 min | **Bake:** 12 min @ 375°F

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Quantity     |
|----------------------|--------------|
| All-purpose flour    | 2 1/4 cups   |
| Baking soda          | 1 tsp        |
| Salt                 | 1 tsp        |
| Unsalted butter      | 1 cup (2 sticks), softened |
| Granulated sugar     | 3/4 cup      |
| Brown sugar (packed) | 3/4 cup      |
| Large eggs           | 2            |
| Vanilla extract      | 2 tsp        |
| Chocolate chips      | 2 cups       |
| Chopped walnuts      | 1 cup (optional) |

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl. Set aside.
3. Beat butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar in a large mixer bowl
   until light and fluffy (about 3 minutes).
4. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in vanilla.
5. Gradually add the flour mixture, mixing on low speed until just combined.
   Do not overmix.
6. Fold in chocolate chips (and walnuts if using) with a spatula.
7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared baking sheets,
   spacing them 2 inches apart.
8. Bake 9–11 minutes or until golden brown around the edges.
   Centers will look slightly underdone — they firm up as they cool.
9. Cool on baking sheets for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.

---

## Flavor Notes & Technique Tips

- **Brown butter variation:** Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat
  until golden and nutty-smelling before cooling and using.
- **Chilling the dough** for 24–72 hours in the refrigerator deepens flavor
  and reduces spread.
- **Sea salt finish:** A pinch of flaky sea salt on top before baking
  amplifies chocolate flavor.
- **Altitude adjustment (>3500 ft):** Reduce baking soda to 3/4 tsp and
  add 2 tbsp extra flour.
