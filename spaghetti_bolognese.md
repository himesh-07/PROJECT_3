# Spaghetti Bolognese

**Source:** Italian Kitchen Classics, Chapter 2
**Yield:** 4 servings | **Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 45 min

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Quantity         |
|-------------------------|------------------|
| Spaghetti                | 400g             |
| Ground beef (80/20)     | 500g             |
| Yellow onion (diced)    | 1 medium         |
| Garlic cloves (minced)  | 4                |
| Carrot (finely diced)   | 1 medium         |
| Celery stalk (diced)    | 2 stalks         |
| Crushed tomatoes        | 1 can (28 oz)    |
| Tomato paste            | 2 tbsp           |
| Dry red wine            | 1/2 cup          |
| Whole milk              | 1/4 cup          |
| Olive oil               | 2 tbsp           |
| Fresh basil             | small bunch      |
| Salt & black pepper     | to taste         |
| Parmesan (grated)       | to serve         |

---

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
   Add onion, carrot, and celery; cook until softened, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more until fragrant.
3. Add ground beef and cook, breaking it up, until no pink remains (6–8 min).
   Season with salt and pepper.
4. Pour in the red wine and simmer until mostly evaporated (~3 minutes).
5. Stir in tomato paste; cook 2 minutes to deepen flavor.
6. Add crushed tomatoes. Reduce heat to low, partially cover, and simmer
   30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Stir in milk during the last 5 minutes of cooking. This tenderizes the
   meat and rounds out acidity.
8. Cook spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until al dente.
   Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
9. Toss pasta with sauce, adding pasta water as needed to loosen.
   Finish with fresh basil and parmesan.

---

## Flavor Notes & Substitutions

- **Ground beef → ground turkey or lentils** for a lighter or vegetarian version.
- **Red wine substitute:** Use 1/4 cup beef stock + 1 tbsp red wine vinegar.
- **Gluten-free:** Swap spaghetti for chickpea or brown rice pasta.
- **Dairy-free:** Omit milk and parmesan; add 1 tsp sugar to balance acidity.
